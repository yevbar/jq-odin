# Facts workstream

Extract behavior, ownership, and lifetime facts from the pinned jq source.

Deliverables:

- cited claims in the appropriate `evidence/claims/*.tsv` shard;
- ownership classifications in `evidence/ownership/*.tsv`;
- unresolved questions stated as questions, not guessed answers;
- two independent adversarial votes before a row becomes `accepted`.

Do not propose Odin APIs while extracting facts.
